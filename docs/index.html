User Guide & Setup

Everything you need to run, configure, and operate Better US Econ Data.

1

Installation & Local Build

Better US Econ Data (BetterEcon) is built for iOS 17.0+ using pure Swift, SwiftUI, and Swift Charts. You can build it directly with Xcode or regenerate the project file using XcodeGen:

git clone https://github.com/sgoley/Better-FRED.git cd Better-FRED xcodegen generate --spec project.yml open BetterEcon.xcodeproj

Select the BetterEcon scheme, pick any iOS 17+ Simulator (e.g. iPhone 16 Pro), and press Cmd + R to run.

2

Getting a Free FRED® API Key

The Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis provides free, permanent API keys to developers, analysts, and students. A key is not required to explore preview data, but unlocks live queries across 800,000+ time series:

  • Visit the St. Louis Fed FRED® Website and create a free account.
  • Go to My Account > API Keys and request a key.
  • Keys are issued immediately as a 32-character hexadecimal string with a generous 120 requests/minute quota.

Development Convenience: Auto-Seeding

During local development, you can place your key in a gitignored .env file (FRED_API_KEY=your_key) or an Xcode scheme environment variable. The app will automatically seed Keychain on first launch!

3

In-App Key Configuration & Live Verification

Better US Econ Data features a dedicated Settings screen accessible from the Settings tab or by tapping the Live API / Preview pill on the Overview dashboard.

  • Masked Key Input: Paste your 32-character key from the clipboard or toggle visibility.
  • Test Connection Button: Performs a live ping against FRED observations before saving, verifying your key is active.
  • Save Key: Safely writes to the iOS Secure Keychain and refreshes your watchlist with live observations.
  • Remove Key: Seamlessly return to offline preview mode at any time.
4

Navigating Charts, Recessions & Zoom

Every series detail screen is equipped with professional inspection controls:

  • Interactive Scrubber: Drag horizontally across any chart to inspect exact readings and period deltas in the floating HUD.
  • NBER Recessions: Toggle gray recession bands spanning 1973 through the 2020 COVID-19 shock.
  • Range Selectors: Switch between 1Y, 5Y, 10Y, and Max time horizons.
  • Step Zoom (+ / -): Dynamically pinch or tap zoom buttons to focus on specific economic cycles.
5

Landscape Trading Desk & Exports

Turn your device sideways for an edge-to-edge landscape workstation. Tap the Share button to export 3x retina PNG images with source attribution, or use the Table mode to copy raw TSV numbers directly into Excel or Numbers.

Privacy & Trust

Zero-Knowledge Architecture

How Better US Econ Data guarantees absolute privacy and direct-to-FRED security.

Hardware-Backed On-Device Storage

When you save your FRED® API key in Better US Econ Data, it is stored using Apple's kSecClassGenericPassword with kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlock accessibility. This stores your credential inside the hardware-backed Secure Enclave on your device.

Direct-to-Fed Network Communication

There are no proxy servers, no intermediate relay nodes, and no analytics collection. All HTTPS requests originate on your device and terminate directly at https://api.stlouisfed.org over encrypted TLS. Neither the developer nor any third party can inspect your search queries or watchlist series.

Apple Privacy Manifest Compliance

The repository bundles an official Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) adhering strictly to App Store requirements:

  • Tracking: false (No tracking domains)
  • Data Collected: None
  • Accessed APIs: NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults declared with reason CA92.1 solely for local display preferences and saved watchlist series IDs.

App Store Verification

Privacy Policy

Official privacy policy for Better US Econ Data (BetterEcon) App Store submission.

Effective Date: September 13, 2026

Better US Econ Data ("BetterEcon") is designed with a privacy-first, zero-knowledge architecture. We believe your economic research and indicator monitoring are strictly your own business.

1. Information Collection and Telemetry

  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide personal information to use the application.
  • Zero Tracking or Analytics: The app contains no third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, crash-reporting telemetry, or device fingerprinting.
  • No Intermediary Server: The app does not operate a backend server. Network requests travel directly from your device to the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.

2. API Key Management

Your 32-character FRED® API key is saved exclusively in your device's iOS Secure Keychain. It is never logged or transmitted to any third-party server, and only sent directly to api.stlouisfed.org over TLS to authorize data requests. You can remove your key at any time.

3. Local Storage

Saved watchlist series IDs and view configurations are stored locally on your device using standard iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

4. Third-Party Privacy Practices

When live data fetching is active, the app communicates with the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis FRED® API. Their data practices are governed by the St. Louis Fed Privacy Policy.

5. Contact & Support

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or need support, please submit an issue on the GitHub repository.
