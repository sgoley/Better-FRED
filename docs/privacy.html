Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Policy for Better US Econ Data (BetterEcon) on iOS.

Effective Date: September 13, 2026


Better US Econ Data ("BetterEcon") is built from the ground up with a zero-knowledge, privacy-first architecture. We believe your economic research, watchlist configurations, and data monitoring should remain strictly confidential.

1. Information Collection and Telemetry

  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, register, or provide an email address to use BetterEcon.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: The app does not include advertising identifiers, third-party analytics frameworks, crash-reporting SDKs, or device fingerprinting.
  • No Intermediary Server: BetterEcon operates without an intermediary relay server. Requests travel directly from your device to the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis over TLS.

2. API Key Management (BYOK)

If you enter your personal Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED®) API key, it is saved exclusively in your device's iOS Secure Keychain (kSecClassGenericPassword with kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlock).

The key is never logged, never transmitted to any third party, and only sent directly to api.stlouisfed.org to authorize your observation requests. You can remove or replace your API key at any time from the in-app Settings screen.

3. Local Storage

Your saved watchlist series and view preferences are stored strictly locally on your device via standard iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

4. Apple Privacy Manifest Adherence

The application includes an Apple Privacy Manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declaring:

  • NSPrivacyTracking: false
  • NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes: [] (Zero data collected)
  • NSPrivacyAccessedAPITypes: NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults with approved reason code CA92.1.

5. Third-Party Services

When live data fetching is active, the app communicates with the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis FRED® API. Their data practices are governed by the St. Louis Fed Privacy Policy.

6. Contact & Support

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or need support, please submit an issue on the GitHub repository.
